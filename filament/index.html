Frequently asked questions

How is my data stored?
Locally on your device. Cloud Backup (Pro) syncs to a hidden folder in your own Google Drive — we never see your data on any server we operate.
What's included in Pro?
Unlimited spools, CSV export and import, cloud backup with photo sync, custom material types, drying log, print cost tracking, and a stats screen. One-time purchase through Google Play — no subscription, ever.
How do notifications work?
We check for low-stock spools (below 15% remaining) at the frequency you choose — Daily, Weekly, or Off. No notification is sent if every spool is fine.
What happens if I uninstall the app?
Local data is deleted. With Cloud Backup enabled, reinstalling and signing in with the same Google account restores your spools automatically.
Can I recover a deleted spool?
Yes. Deleted spools are kept for 30 days. Open History and tap Restore on the spool you want back.
