Filament is an Android app for tracking 3D printing filament inventory, developed and maintained by Bruno Santos, trading as The Cynic Dev ("we", "us"). It helps you track spool stock, log prints, and get low-stock notifications.

Filament began as a personal tool and is shared publicly as a useful utility. It is maintained on a best-effort basis by one person.
