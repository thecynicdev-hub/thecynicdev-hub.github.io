Privacy Policy

Filament is a filament inventory tracker for 3D printing. Your spool data lives on your device, and — only if you choose to turn on Cloud Backup — in your own Google Drive. We do not run servers that store your spools, your prints, or anything else about you.

Short version: Filament stores all your data locally on your device. If you enable Cloud Backup (Pro), your data syncs to a hidden folder in your own Google Drive that only Filament can see — we cannot access it, and neither can any other app. We collect no analytics, no telemetry, and no personal data beyond what Google Play collects when you make a purchase.

1. Who we are

Filament is developed and maintained by Bruno Santos, trading as The Cynic Dev ("we", "us", "our"). For questions about this policy, email us at the address at the bottom of this page.

2. What data Filament stores

Filament stores the following data on your device, in the app's local database:

  • Spool details you enter — name, brand, material, colour, diameter, weight remaining
  • Print history you log against each spool — job name, grams used, date, notes
  • Drying log entries (Pro) — temperature and duration per session
  • Photos you attach to a spool (Pro) — picked via Android's built-in photo picker
  • App settings — weight unit, currency symbol, notification frequency, theme

None of this data is sent to us. It stays on your device unless you turn on Cloud Backup, described below.

3. What data we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect analytics or usage telemetry
  • We do not use crash reporting or third-party tracking SDKs
  • We do not run any server that receives or stores your spool data
  • We do not sell or share your data with any third party
  • We show no ads and use no ad networks

4. Cloud Backup and Google Drive (Pro feature)

Cloud Backup is optional and off by default. If you turn it on and sign in with your Google account, Filament requests exactly one Google Drive permission: drive.appdata — access to a single hidden folder ("app data folder") that Google creates specifically for Filament inside your own Drive storage.

  • This folder counts against your own Drive storage quota, not ours
  • It does not appear in your regular Drive file list and cannot be opened by any other app
  • We — the developers — have no access to this folder or its contents. Nothing about it is visible to us at any point
  • Your device talks directly to Google's servers to read and write this folder; no request passes through anything we operate

You can revoke this access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access or uninstalling Filament does not delete data already stored in the folder — sign back in with Cloud Backup enabled to resume, or contact Google to clear leftover app data for your account.

5. Photo attachments (Pro feature)

When you attach a photo to a spool, Filament opens Android's built-in Photo Picker — the system picker Google provides so apps never need broad access to your photo library. Filament only receives the single photo you choose. The photo is stored locally and, if Cloud Backup is on, also uploaded (compressed) to the same hidden Drive folder described above.

6. Payments and Google Play

Filament Pro is a one-time purchase made through Google Play Billing. Google acts as the merchant of record for this transaction: Google collects your payment details and processes the charge directly. We receive confirmation that a purchase was made but never see or store your payment card details.

Google's privacy policy governs how they handle your payment data: policies.google.com/privacy. Refund requests are handled by Google Play directly — see the Google Play refund policy.

7. Low-stock notifications

Filament can notify you when a spool is running low. This check runs entirely on your device on a schedule you control in Settings — it does not contact any server, and no data leaves your device to generate this notification.

8. Permissions we request and why

  • Notifications — to show the low-stock alert described above
  • Internet — used only for Cloud Backup (talking to Google Drive) and Google Play Billing (processing your purchase). Filament makes no other network requests

9. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Deleting a spool moves it to a recycle bin for 30 days (so you can restore it from the History screen), after which it is permanently removed from your device and, if Cloud Backup is on, from your Drive folder on the next sync.

  • Uninstalling Filament removes all locally stored data from your device
  • Revoking Drive access (see section 4) stops future syncing but does not retroactively delete what's already in your Drive folder

10. Children's privacy

Filament is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be noted with an updated date at the top of this page and a new row in the change history above. Continued use of Filament after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact
