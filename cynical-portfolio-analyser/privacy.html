Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the Cynical Portfolio Analyser web application. For ContextShift's privacy policy, see ContextShift Privacy Policy.

Short version: We process portfolio URLs, resume content, and code you share to generate analysis reports. Your content is sent to third-party AI providers. Generated reports are stored permanently by their unique URL. We have no accounts, no tracking cookies, and no mailing list.

What we collect

  • URLs you submit for portfolio analysis
  • Content of resumes (PDF text extracted server-side) you upload for analysis
  • GitHub repository URLs you submit
  • Generated reports — stored permanently, accessible only by their unique URL
  • A SHA-256 hash of your IP address for rate limiting only — the original IP is never stored
  • License key and instance ID — stored in your browser's localStorage if you go Pro, never sent to our servers beyond the validation request

What we don't collect

  • No analytics, no telemetry, no behaviour tracking
  • No tracking cookies — only essential cookies for the Lemon Squeezy checkout flow
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No email addresses — we have no mailing list
  • No user accounts — we don't have signup or login

Third parties

The Cynical Portfolio Analyser sends data to third-party services to deliver the product:

  • Groq Inc. (primary AI provider) — receives scraped portfolio text, resume text, or repository data to generate the analysis. Groq's free tier may use submitted data for service improvement. See: groq.com/privacy-policy
  • Google Generative AI / Gemini (fallback AI provider) — same data, used only when Groq is unavailable. Google's free tier may use data for AI training. See: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Upstash Inc. (database) — stores generated reports keyed by UUID. See: upstash.com/trust/privacy.pdf
  • Vercel Inc. (hosting) — handles HTTP requests, may log standard access information. See: vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Lemon Squeezy (payments and license management, our Merchant of Record) — collects billing information directly. We do not see your full payment details. See: lemonsqueezy.com/privacy

How long we keep data

  • Shareable reports: stored indefinitely, accessible only by their unique URL. You can request deletion by emailing thecynicdev@gmail.com with the report URL.
  • URL analysis cache: 7 days, then automatically deleted.
  • Rate limiting hash: the free tier counter is permanent per IP hash. The Pro counter resets daily.

Your rights

You can:

  • Request deletion of any specific report
  • Request information about what data we hold about you
  • Stop using the product at any time

Email thecynicdev@gmail.com for any privacy request. We aim to respond within 7 days.

Children

This product is not intended for users under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

International users

We are based in Portugal (EU). By using the product, you consent to data transfers to the third-party services listed above, some of which are based outside the EU.

For EU users, we process data under the legitimate interest basis (providing the requested service) and the contract basis (delivering paid products).

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. Material changes will be announced on the hub site (thecynicdev-hub.github.io). Continued use constitutes acceptance.
