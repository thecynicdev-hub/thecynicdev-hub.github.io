Not satisfied for any reason? Request a full refund within 14 days of purchase. No questions asked. No conditions. No usage limits.

Refunds are handled by Lemon Squeezy
Cynical Portfolio Analyser Pro is sold by Lemon Squeezy, who act as the Merchant of Record for all purchases. They manage payment processing, receipts, and refunds on our behalf. You do not need to contact us directly to get a refund.

How to request a refund

  1. Find your Lemon Squeezy receipt email — sent to the address you used at checkout immediately after purchase. Your order number is in the subject line.
  2. Click the "Request refund" link in the receipt email, OR visit the Lemon Squeezy customer portal linked from that email, OR email support@lemonsqueezy.com referencing your order number.
  3. Lemon Squeezy will process your refund. It typically appears on your statement within 5–10 business days depending on your bank or card provider.

After a refund

When a refund is processed:

  • Your license key is automatically deactivated
  • You lose access to Pro features immediately
  • Any reports you generated remain accessible by their unique URL — we do not delete them automatically. Email thecynicdev@gmail.com if you want a specific report removed.

Outside the 14-day window

After 14 days, refunds are at our discretion. If you have an unusual situation — accidental purchase, double charge, or similar — contact us at thecynicdev@gmail.com and we'll consider it case-by-case.

In case of product discontinuation

  • We provide at least 60 days notice before shutdown
